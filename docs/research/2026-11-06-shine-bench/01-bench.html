Tip

This post is shiny. Tilt your phone and the light moves across it, like tilting a real foil card.

The setting and the tip are draft copy for the glass and the onboarding tips, which the owner approves in review.

What to try

  • Feelin' Fab, three grades of foil. One is a pearl sheen, two is holo bands that slide with the light, and three is cracked-ice prism holo with starbursts. Each starburst flashes when the light lines up with it.
  • Glitter Bomb. Thousands of rainbow foil flakes, each tilted its own way, so only a few catch the light at once. The silver ones are holographic and flash whatever hue their tilt throws. The bomb puffs and the flakes tumble down and land. Faster mouse moves throw more flashes.
  • Glitter gets everywhere. Pass the mouse over the bombed post and it picks up flakes. They drop off over whatever you pass next. That glitter is only on your screen and gone on reload.
  • Dark board. Switch to it, then compare the three contrast choices. Turn on "Show the scrim" and tune its pad and feather.
  • Reduce motion. The light is held where it rests, the bomb has already landed, and nothing clings.

Not in this mockup

  • A real gyroscope. The artifact frame can't read one, so the pad stands in for it.
  • Drawing only near the viewport, the shared observer, and the frame budget on a busy page. The plan sets those.
  • The card faces' foil. The same light and holo would serve a foil variant on a postcard later.
  • Here the contrast is computed per pixel from the board's tokens. The real check would sample the material across every light angle.
