Heads Will Roll bench

The post's reactions have come loose. Each head is a live tile: press one to react with it (pending for the grace, press again to undo). A head's mass is its count, an Overreaction head's times the multiple, and the light ones fall round the heavy ones. Press +1 on the counts to watch a head grow. To measure, start Catch trials: a gold ring marks a head, and the time from the ring to your press is recorded against that head's size and speed. Those times set the reel-in a key or switch press takes.

Catches

Each pointer catch in a trial, by head. Speed ÷ size is the head's average speed over its diameter while ringed: how many of its own widths it travels a second. The reel-in for a key press on a head is the median of measured catches within ±30% of its speed ÷ size, or the formula when there are fewer than three.

  • The heads fill the content area border to border: its borders are their walls. The reactions row under the post is the bench's control (it is empty on the real card except for this note's counts).
  • Tab reaches the heads in order of mass, heaviest first; Enter or Space on a head starts its reel-in, a second press cancels it. Pointer presses land at once.
  • Space: the box is as wide as the content area and about as deep as it is wide, seen from the side and tilted down. A head's size on screen is its size in the box over its distance, and the heads are drawn far to near, so an orbit passes behind its sun and in front of it. A satellite goes to the sun nearest it when the suns are counted; the nearer orbits go to the heavier satellites, and each orbit's pace follows its sun's mass and its radius, as a planet's does.
  • Reduced motion stills the heads in loose places; a key press and a pointer press both reel in, for the same time.
