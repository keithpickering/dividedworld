# The Graffiti design session's mockup

Record, written 2026-11-11. The mockup the Graffiti design session (`docs/plans/2026-11-11-graffiti-design.md`) drew
with the owner. It is one self-contained HTML fragment that loads Anton and Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts.

- `01-tool-styles.html`: the Draw button and the drawing toolbar in four styles, on a 360px phone, at the same moment
  (a wall at 31% of its 40% limit, a reader drawing in cyan). The styles are A, the '03 forum (an oekaki applet's
  chrome); B, the glass with flat color chips; C, skeuomorphic (a metal tray and glossy keys); and D, the glass with
  drawn cans. The owner chose D.

What the mockup draws that the design changed afterward:

- The caps read Fine, Marker and Fat cap. The owner said the names "imply pens", so the design names them Skinny,
  Stock and Fat.
- The ink is drawn as flat lines. The design draws spray paint: a core, overspray and speckle.
- There is no Stencils tab. The design adds one (section 9).
