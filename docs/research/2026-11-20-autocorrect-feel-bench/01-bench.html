Autocorrect feel bench

Two questions from the design session: how the corner looks while the author holds an end to pick it, and how strong the adhesive left by a peeled strip should be. The tape under both is the card's own paper.

1. The corner fold

The torn corner folds back along a diagonal crease, so the fold's ragged edge is the end's own. The fold shows the tape's sticky underside (lighter, with a sheen), it casts a shadow, and the page shows in the gap. As the pick goes on, the crease moves in and the corner stands up. When the pick is up, the crease runs across the whole end, which is where the roll starts. "Today" is the build's current triangle, for comparison.

2. Hold to pick, with the buzz

Press near the left end and hold still. The corner lifts over 1000ms. When it is up the phone buzzes, and from then on a drag would peel the strip for good. Move more than 8px before that and it becomes a peek instead. Vibration works on Android browsers. iOS Safari offers no vibration API to web pages, so an iPhone gets the corner and no buzz.

3. The adhesive

A half-peeled strip on each board at four film strengths. "Today" is what the build draws: the tint at full strength under a flat gloss, which reads as more tape. "Bench" is what the tape bench was approved at (light .48, dark .30, a gloss only near the light). Move the pointer over a board to move the light.
