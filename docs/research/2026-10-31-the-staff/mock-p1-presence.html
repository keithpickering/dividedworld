The three places phpBB put it. Each lists the masks active there in the past 5 minutes (an instance setting), refreshed by a 30-second poll that is also the viewer's heartbeat. A mask appears on its first poll and leaves only by ageing out, so a swap never shows "A left, B arrived" together. Up to 15 names (a setting), then "and N others". Names link to profiles, with no avatars (tried with square minis; the owner: they make the list too messy). Your own current mask is listed for you; if it is hidden it shows to you alone, in italics.
