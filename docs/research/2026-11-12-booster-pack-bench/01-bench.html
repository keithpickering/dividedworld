What this draft leaves open

  • The render has its own outline: fine crimp teeth and a transparent ground, in place of the engine's ten clipped zigzags.
  • The coin is printed and embossed in the render. The name and the count are not on it yet.
  • The highlight uses a second picture of the scene (which way each bit of film faces), so it catches the wrinkles. A flat sweep would need no second picture and would not.
  • It is a draft: the final would be 1080 by 1680 at full samples, with the film's folds and print worked over.
