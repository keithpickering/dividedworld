Classes

Every character has one, and only you can see it. A class decides which corners of the reaction grid pay your character and which ones cost it. Reactions to a card you play count for you too, read through your class.

Pick the one that sounds like how you already post, or one that sounds like a bad idea. You can Turn later.

Top row excited, bottom calm; left negative, right positive. + earns · − hurts

+−
FACE

The crowd's favorite. Earns from excited, positive reactions, and from the cheers a post draws after its Sweet card lands there. Hurt by bored reactions, and by a card that outdraws the post it was played on.

GABE'S HINTGet behind the posts you like.

+−
HEEL

Earns heat: excited, negative reactions, and a Salty card that outdraws the post it lands on. Hurt by the polite shrug, calm and positive.

GABE'S HINTBeing hated is a living.

−+
JABRONI

Anti-comedy: posts built to bomb. Earns from bored, negative reactions and from Salty cards others land on its posts. Hurt by being taken sincerely, excited and positive.

GABE'S HINTCommit to the bit. Especially when nobody laughs.

−+
TECHNICIAN

The respected regular. Earns from calm, positive reactions to its posts and cards, and more when an old post of its own still draws them. Hurt by heat: excited, negative reactions.

GABE'S HINTWrite the reply people come back to.
