What every option does the same way

  • The spinner takes the color of what it sits on: neon on a tab, ink on a primary button.
  • A button keeps its width: label and spinner share one cell, and only their opacity changes.
  • The tab's spinner slides in beside its label; the tab stays where it is until the screen has landed.
  • With Motion Off the spinner stands still and lit, and the screen reader hears the page is busy.
