# The onboarding design session's boards

Record, written 2026-11-02. The boards the onboarding design session drew with the owner, in the style of
`docs/research/2026-10-29-two-worlds/` and `docs/research/2026-11-02-alerts/`: each is one self-contained HTML file at
a fixed size, loading Anton and Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts. They are references for the look, not code to
copy. Their copy is a draft for the owner's review.

- `01-welcome.html`: the welcome page a new account opens on once: Gabe's letter, Before you start (theme, motion,
  text size), and Make your first character.
- `02-guide.html`: the Strategy Guide, scrolled to Classes: the contents in view, each class drawn from its class
  card's entry, and Gabe's hint.
- `03-spotlight.html`: step 2 of the HUD tour over the board: the page dimmed and blurred, a cut-out around the orbs
  with the welcome gift's orb, and Gabe's tip panel.
- `assets/gabe-sprite.gif`: the owner's Gabe sprite. `assets/gabe-avatar.png` is its head and shoulders, cropped to a
  210px square, scaled 2x nearest-neighbor onto a violet matte.
- `04-phone.html`: three phone screens at 390 by 844: the HUD tour's last step as a sheet docked above the HUD with
  the cut-out over Hand, the welcome page stacked, and the guide with its contents folded into a menu.
