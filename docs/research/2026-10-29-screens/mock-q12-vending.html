Q12. The Vending Machine, and your hand in the HUD

Buying is private: nobody sees what you bought or hold; only a play is a public Act. A card is bought with solid (settled) orbs only. Your hand is the player's, shared by all your characters, as the core-loop design set.

A. A drawn machine (recommended)

The page is a vending machine: a game piece, so it is in color, like the orbs. Each slot is a real button ("Buy Stick Around, 1.00 orb"); pressing it shows the price on the little display, takes the orbs from your HUD (the cascade runs backwards), and drops the card into the tray, from where it flies into your hand. With reduced motion the card simply appears in the hand. Sizes are percentages of the machine, which is a percentage of the screen.
