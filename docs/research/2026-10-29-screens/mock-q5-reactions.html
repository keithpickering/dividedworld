Q5. Reactions and actions under a post

Everyone sees the tallies; nobody sees who reacted. Your own reactions are marked (bold, outlined), and only on your screen. You react as the character in your HUD. A post by one of your own characters shows its tallies but no react button, since the server would refuse it, and your screen already knows which characters are yours.

A. Emoji chips, and a picker laid out as the four quadrants (recommended)

Tallies are chips with counts, in the order they arrived. The picker is a 2×2 grid shaped like the circumplex: excited at the top, calm at the bottom, negative left, positive right, as the spec places them (the Heel's heat top-left, the Face's hype top-right), so the game's map is the control itself, and a new player learns the quadrants by reacting. "2 of 3 left" counts across all your characters on this post. Arrow keys move through the grid; each button's name is the emoji's name and quadrant.

B. Chips plus a quadrant heat square

A as before, plus a tiny 2×2 square at the start of the row, shaded by each quadrant's share of the vote, so how the crowd took a post reads at a glance across a page. It is the "heatmap of how the crowd responded" from the core-loop design, drawn literally. It adds a colored game piece to every post, and its shading is the vote, which a reader could watch move when they react.
