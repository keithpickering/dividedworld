Stick Around: prototype, round 2

Two Stick Arounds this time, #15 and #30. Once one has been on screen it can hold you; when you stop scrolling, the nearest one you have seen and not escaped pulls you back: slowly at first, then faster, landing with a rubber band. Escape it by ticking its certification, by struggling (scrolling hard and repeatedly: on a phone, one fling is not enough), or with its Struggle button (ten presses within two and a half seconds). Jumps (End, Home, a link) never count as struggling. The tuning panel lets you set how hard breaking free is; tell me the numbers that feel right on your phone. Reset holds you again.
