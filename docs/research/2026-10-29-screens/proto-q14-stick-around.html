Stick Around: prototype, round 6 (the slingshot, read along the arc)

Two Stick Arounds, #15 and #30. When you stop scrolling, the nearest one you have seen and not escaped pulls you back, slowly at first and landing with a rubber band. To break free, fire the slingshot.

  • On a touch screen: drag far enough one way to cock it (the frame stretches; no hurry, it stays cocked while your finger stays down and you do not drift back), then flick fiercely the other way to fire.
  • With a mouse: grab the handle on the frame (⇕), drag it back to cock, flick it the other way and let go to fire; the page launches. The wheel still breaks free with round 4's flick: a quick nudge one way, then at once a fierce scroll the other.
  • Keyboard and screen reader: the BREAK FREE button: press once to wind up, again between 0.12 and 0.6 seconds later to release.

A hard scroll on its own never breaks free. Certifying releases for good. The bar at the bottom reads out each attempt; the tuning panel sets what each path needs. Reset holds you again.
