A. The classic user bar (recommended)

The phpBB "Welcome back" row, turned into the game's bar: Posting as [character ▾], the orbs, then the links. On a phone the bar sticks to the top and the links fold into a Menu button. The quick-swap is a plain <select>, so it works with a keyboard and a screen reader as it is.

B. A TTYD HUD docked at the bottom

The forum header stays a plain 2003 banner with links; the game rides in a HUD fixed to the bottom of the screen (avatar, character, orbs), like a game's status bar. Thumb-reachable on a phone, and always on screen while reading. It costs a strip of every screen, and it is the least 2003 of the three.

C. Only where it is used

No game bar. The character is chosen in the composer (and beside reactions and card plays); the orbs live on the Vending Machine and the log.
