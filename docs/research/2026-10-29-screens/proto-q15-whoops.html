Whoops!: prototype

Drag on the ink to scrub. As a reader your scrubbing is yours alone and a refresh brings the ink back; switch to "the author" and your strokes are saved for everyone (here, in this page's memory), either washing or writing back in the ink. "New splat" plays the card again with a new seed: every play looks different, and every reader of one play sees the same splat. Drag the width slider to see the post at phone and desktop widths: the text, the splat and the author's strokes keep their places. "Splat again on top" stacks another play (up to three) in the color now in the picker: a stroke scrubs the top layer where it starts, so you work down through the colors one at a time (the owner's choice; "every layer" is kept to compare).
