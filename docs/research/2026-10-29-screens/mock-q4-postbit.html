Q4. The postbit: what a post shows about its character

The left column of every post on a desktop; on a phone it folds into a header row above the post. Everything here is public. The question is how the character's season standing (its settled Aura this season) shows. The reactions and actions row is the next question, so it is only a placeholder here.

One mask point applies to all three: the postbit says "Joined Oct 2026" (the month, never the time), because two characters created a minute apart is a way of reading who owns them.

A. The number, with a gauge

"Aura this season: 14.20" and a thin gauge against the season's top standing. Exact, so accusation threads can cite it, and it reads like a score. The most information, and the most like a leaderboard.
