ACCESSIBILITY

These change how the board and the glass look, read and move. They never change how hard a card is to get through.

MOTION

Reduced stops the drifting smoke, the pulses and the orb shine. A card's effect stays: Stick Around still snaps you back, only instantly.

SMOKE

How much cloud sits behind the glass. Off leaves plain black. Text is readable at every setting.

CONTRAST

High puts solid black behind every line of text on the glass and lifts it all to at least 7:1. The smoke stays at the edges of the screen.

TEXT SIZE

Applies to the board and the glass together. Past 150% the glass folds into one column, and nothing is cut off.

THE BOARD

The 2003 board stays 2003 either way. Only its two colors swap.

READ-OUTS

A screen reader hears one short line when a card lands, like "Whoops! splatted this post. Scrub it with the keyboard."

Changes save as you make them.
