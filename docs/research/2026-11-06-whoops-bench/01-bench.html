What to try

  • Suds. Bubbles with a rainbow film that drift and pop, lather that stays where you scrubbed and slowly breaks up, and dirty water that runs down the post.
  • Reactions row. Wipe beside the chips. Then pick "Full width (today)" and outline it to see the strip nobody can wipe.
  • Sponge. With a mouse over ink you get a dripping sponge. It squishes when you press. Over the reactions, or a link you've uncovered, the normal cursor comes back.
  • Splat. Every join is rounded now. "New splat" draws another seed.
  • On a phone. A quick swipe up or down scrolls the page. A sideways swipe wipes. Press and hold for a moment, then wipe in any direction.
