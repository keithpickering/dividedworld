# Minus World's spellbooks

Record, written 2026-10-28. Four spreadsheets the owner kept from Minus World, the forum whose second version (2015)
first added the gameplay Divided World grows from. "Spells" were the precursor to postcards. They do not match
exactly what shipped on the site: some did, others are in the same spirit but were too complex at the time. The owner
supplied them in the core-loop design session (`docs/plans/2026-10-28-core-loop-design.md`).

- `season-1-spellbook-roster.csv`: the Season 1 roster with alignments (Sweet, Salty, Pepperoni), prices, build
  status, and spell combinations.
- `season-2-spellbook-roster.csv`: new spells planned for Season 2.
- `spellbook-levels.csv`: an earlier draft by level, with each spell's alert message.
- `user-spells.csv`: spells cast on a user rather than a post.

What the design took from them: the three alignments; "you absorbed half the EXP" when a Salty or Pepperoni spell
landed on you (kept as a class trick, not a universal rule); spells that know about each other (combinations);
effects that wear off or never block reading ("Level 1 spells don't restrict access to posts in any way"); and two
cards for the first slice, Stick Around and Whoops!. The goal is to bring back Minus World's greatest hits over time,
at various rarities, and not to be limited by them.

The owner's two later prototypes, which CodePen would not serve to the design session's container:
the Whoops! ink splat (https://codepen.io/keithpickering/pen/mdpKrbm) and the TTYD Aura bar
(https://codepen.io/keithpickering/pen/QwbLYyp).
