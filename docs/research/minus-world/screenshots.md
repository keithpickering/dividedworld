# Minus World, as it looked

Record, written 2026-10-30. Five screenshots of Minus World the owner supplied as an inspiration board, "random slice of
life" from the forum's second version, in `docs/research/minus-world/screenshots/`. Below is what each shows, and what
each suggests for Divided World. Each suggestion is an idea, not a decision.

## What they show

- **`inventory-desktop.png`**: the Inventory page, with tabs for Items, Gear and Knick-Knacks, and a second tab, Equip/Remove
  Gear. The items are worn on the avatar: Baseball Cap, Combover in Chief, the "I Really Wish I Weren't Here Right Now"
  button and the Nostalgic Hat. Each has a flavor line, a quantity and a USE button. Below are panels for Recently Active
  and Recently Consumed. The top bar carries Forum, Member List, Inventory, Spells, Calendar and Strategy Guide.
- **`something-happened-ex-on-the-ground-phone.png`** and **`something-happened-wall-phone.png`**: a purple "SOMETHING
  HAPPENED!" banner over the board index, with its own art: "You found 100 EX on the ground. Good shit." and "You find a
  dick on the wall." Random events, one worth EX and one worth nothing but the joke.
- **`board-index-phone.png`** and **`board-index-desktop-2018.png`**: the board index, grouped under category headers
  (Mayhem Whereabouts, Treasured Pastimes, Event Horizon, Other) with threads, posts, last post and "(1 user browsing)".
  Boards' names changed over time and kept their initials: Positive Pants Palace became Parsimonious Penny Prairie.
  Event Horizon held the Killing Game Grounds, "where the randomly held Minus World Killing Game takes place, when it is
  active".
- **Every page's user box**: a greeting that changes ("Make us proud," "Welcome to the party," "Good to see you," "Stay
  with us forever,"), the level and an EX bar whose color changes with it, and a multiplier (1.00, 1.01x, 2.27x).

## What they suggest

- **Gear worn on a character's avatar** is a kind of item beside cards, and the Recently Consumed panel shows items were
  used up. Under "A response never proves a private fact", a rare hat seen on two characters is a clue, not proof,
  because a hand belongs to the player and every mask draws from it.
- **"Something happened!" is The System at its smallest:** an arbitrary event on an ordinary page load, sometimes worth
  Aura and sometimes only a joke. Any Aura it gives lands in the player's own log ("The fiction may lie; the log never
  does") and stays under the wallet's cap.
- **Boards renamed with their initials kept** is a pattern the weekly turnover could follow: the Director gives the
  board a new name and the regulars keep calling it by its letters.
- **An event board for a game held now and then** (the Killing Game) is a board that opens and closes with an event:
  a deduction game inside a deduction game.
- **"(1 user browsing)"** is presence, which the core-loop design held back because a count of players can show that
  two masks are one (`docs/plans/2026-10-28-core-loop-design.md`, section 11). Under the clue rule, a count of
  characters browsing would be a clue; the design still has to be made.
- **The changing greeting** and the bar's color are the user box's flavor, for the HUD's design.
