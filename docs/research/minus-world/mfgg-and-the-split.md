# MFGG, the Pointless Post Palace and The Split

Record, written 2026-10-30. The history behind Divided World's community, read from the MFGG wiki (wiki.mfgg.net) on
that date, with the owner's own account from the same conversation. The pages read were "The Split", "Minus World",
and "Pointless Post Palace". The wiki is the community's own and is quoted as it stood; where it and the owner
differ, both are given.

## The Pointless Post Palace

MFGG's spam and off-topic board, from 2001 to 2007, "dedicated entirely to spam, off-topic posting, and general
thoughtlessness". It was one of the board's oldest and largest sections, and nearly brought down the board's database
around August 2005, after which it was pruned whenever it neared 1,000 pages. Moderators and administrators argued
for years about whether it should exist at all: some wanted it gone to "decrease the stupidity"; some members came to
MFGG only for it.

**It was renamed again and again, by hand.** The wiki lists 27 names, among them Pointless Post SATAN SATAN SATAN SATAN
SATAN (to mark 666 pages), Mike Jones Post Palace (with Mike Jones's voice playing in the background), ZION, THE WAR IS
OVER (a WWII background and a Queen song), Pointless Toast Palace, Pointless Ghost Palace, Pirate Puns Palace, Pointless
WIP Palace (after a pruning, drawn with giant cracks and patches), and "Hell", with banner and background to match.
Land of the Hoff (May 2007): an admin's "Road Work Ahead" sign was replaced by David Hasselhoff images that overlapped
threads and blocked replies, and some members took the Hoff for the road work.

**Its moderation was itself a game.** A read-only Worthless Post Palace held unworthy topics, until it "was found to be
worthless itself". Its name was kept alive after it closed in 2007: the Casual Conversation Castle replaced it "to keep
the triple letter thing going"; it came back in 2015 as the Premium Post Palace (an April Fools joke, a one-day free
trial, then fifteen Tusk Bucks a week), as the Poltergeistic Post Ponderous that October, and as the All That Post
Palace in April 2016. The wiki's search also names a "Banned from PPP" group and the Joliet State Penitentiary, a forum
misbehaving members were sent to; their pages were not read.

## The Split

In 2010 MFGG had been in a long slump. On November 5, 2010, shortly after a change of administrators, DJ Yoshiman and
Black Squirrel proposed separating the fangame part of the forums from the community part. Almost half of members were
for it and slightly more than half against. The community forum, named The Minus World after some debate, opened on
November 15, 2010; the new MFGG forums opened on November 26. The wiki: "Since The Split, MFGG is less active, but the
fangaming-related boards are in a healthier state than they were before splitting." The owner's account: MFGG's
admins decided nothing but Mario fangames should be discussed, which effectively banished those who disagreed to a
backup forum, Minus World.

## Minus World

The wiki calls it "a community-centric forum and web-based fighting game". Kyori ran it until August 2012, and
activity dwindled for about six years. Spritanium proposed moving it from phpBB to MyBB, and the new Minus World opened
on September 11, 2016, meant to be "just as much of a game as it is a forum" and to celebrate mid-2000s internet
culture, run by Draku and Elyk with Spritanium leading development. Its screenshots are in `screenshots.md`.

The owner, on what it did with the old board's name: every week the board took a random "P + P + P" name, "a
rudimentary System" (Positive Pants Palace and Parsimonious Penny Prairie are two). Its Random Events ("Something
happened!") were inspired by Neopets.

## What it suggests

- **The System has a lineage.** The PPP was renamed by hand for years, and Minus World made the renaming random and
  weekly. Divided World's Director renaming its weekly boards is the third version of the same joke. A board that
  keeps its initials while its name changes is the house style.
- **Moderation was played, not only enforced:** a board whose existence was argued over for years, a read-only
  dungeon that turned out to be worthless itself, a jail forum, a ban from one board, a paid premium return. Each
  could be one of The System's effects ("The System acts; it never destroys what a player wrote"), with each naming
  its way out ("No player is ever stuck").
- **The Split is the community's founding story:** a board's rulers deciding what may be talked about, and the people
  they banished building somewhere else. "Divided World" names it.
