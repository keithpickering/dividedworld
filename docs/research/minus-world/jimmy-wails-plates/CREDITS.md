# Credits for the Jimmy Wails banner plates

Record, written 2026-11-12. The photographs behind the files in this folder, found on Wikimedia Commons on that date
and matched by eye. `README.md` beside this file is the earlier record of what the files are and is left as written.

Every file here shows a photograph of Jimmy Wales that the Wikimedia Foundation published under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), and each file is an
adaptation under that same license.

- `blue-shirt.png` and `reference-blue-shirt-original.png`: photograph by Lane Hartwell on behalf of the Wikimedia
  Foundation, 2010, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jimmy_Wales_July_2010.jpg. Cropped and faded for the
  Foundation's banner; the plate has the banner's text painted out.
- `close-up.png` and `reference-close-up-original.webp`: photograph by Lane Hartwell on behalf of the Wikimedia
  Foundation, 2010, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jimmy_Wales_July_2010_4.jpg. Cropped and faded for the
  Foundation's banner; the plate has the banner's text painted out.
- `dark-logo.png` and `reference-german-portal.png`: photograph by Nicolas Goldberg, 2009,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikimania_2009_GOLDBERGN-9398.jpg. Cropped for the Foundation's banner
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jimmy_Appeal_2009.jpg); the plate has the banner's text painted out.

The references also carry the Wikimedia Foundation's own appeal text and layout.
