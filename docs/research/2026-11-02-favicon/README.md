# The favicon design session's sheets and art

Record, written 2026-11-02. What the favicon design session (`docs/plans/2026-11-02-favicon-design.md`) showed the
owner, one sheet per question, and the two pieces of art the owner approved. The sheets are references; the two art
files are the sources the plan (`docs/plans/2026-11-02-favicon-plan.md`) copies into the instance.

- `q1-concepts.png`: four first concepts at 16, 32, 64 and 128px, on light and dark, and in a tab. The owner narrowed
  to A (the division sign in Minus World's pinwheel circle) and B (a coin split between the board and the glass), and
  asked for B with its seam turned flat, "so the separation between the two halves is itself the suggestion of the
  flat line in the division symbol. Sort of like a yin-yang with a flat line instead of a wavy one."
- `q2-flat-seam.png`: B with a flat seam, four ways. The owner chose B1, the seam as a cyan line alone (the black-bar
  versions read as a Poké Ball).
- `q3-pixel-pass.png`: B1 hand-placed at 16px, board on top and glass on top, beside the vector shrunk to 16px.
- `q4-owner-sprite.png`: the owner's own 16px sprite of B1, with transparent and half-transparent pixels on its rim so
  it holds on light and dark tabs, shown on five grounds and in tabs.
- `q5-vector-beside-sprite.png`: the sprite beside the B1 vector at 32px and at home-screen size, and the vector
  redrawn in the sprite's colors. The owner kept the vector as drawn: "sprites sometimes need differences in scaling,
  colors etc to evoke the same appearance of the larger version, which was my goal here".
- `coin-16.png`: the owner's 16px sprite, as drawn. The 16px icon, byte for byte.
- `coin.svg`: B1, the vector every larger size is rendered from, with its seam on whole pixels at 32px.
