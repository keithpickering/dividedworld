# Lane A: The Old Guard and how MFGG was governed

Record, written 2026-10-30, from the MFGG wiki as read on that date. What was read, and how people are treated, is in
`README.md` here; The System's voice, drawn from all four lanes, is in `system-voice.md`.

Source: a local dump of the MFGG wiki. This covers the 114 pages in lane A's page list, plus the governance pages from other lanes that were needed to cover this subject. Each item cites its page title or titles in brackets. The "System:" line under each item says how it could inform The System's behavior or voice.

Quotes are verbatim, so they keep the wiki's own spelling, including its British spellings and typos. Staff appear only by public role and by what they did as staff. Personal details are left out. Some member pages hold them; those pages were read, but none of that detail is copied here. An item that centers on one non-staff individual is marked **(individual: owner's call)**.

An infamous incident (a ban, a dupe caught, a troll, a flamewar, phishing, a hacked account, someone mocked, a member called infamous, a game ridiculed) names no individual, member or staff. It is written in the abstract: what happened in general terms, what the community felt about it, and what that suggests for the design. A page title that is a person's name is cited as [a member page]. Event accounts are described by what they did, not by their usernames.

## 1. Voice and attitude: quotes

### 1.1 The Submissions FAQ: the bureaucrat who will not be moved

The single best voice sample in the dump. It is curt, in capitals, never reverses itself, and contains one absurd entry.

- "Wait. All submissions are dealt with eventually. The site staff have lives and cannot guarantee to be working on MFGG's website every day." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: a stock reply for an appeal that is still waiting. It is unhurried and admits no obligation.
- "DO NOT SEND MESSAGES TO THE SITE STAFF ABOUT THIS ISSUE. DO NOT CREATE DUPLICATE SUBMISSIONS. DOING EITHER WILL SLOW THE SUBMISSION PROCESS FURTHER." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: re-filing or nagging an appeal is a punishable act. This is in character, but the bound has to be stated in the log.
- "Submissions which remain in the queue for a long time are MEDIOCRE. MEDIOCRITY IS A BAD THING." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: The System may read its own delay as a verdict ("it sat too long, so it was mediocre"). That is a bluff the fiction can tell.
- "If they are being declined quickly, this shows you are not THINKING before submitting" [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: a fast rejection comes with a sneer about effort.
- "We are not responsible for making your submissions better. It is entirely YOUR responsibility to make your submission appealing to us" [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: The System gives no coaching. The burden is always on the player.
- "My submission was declined, will you reconsider your stance on it? No." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: the canonical one-word ruling on a second appeal. "No." is a full verdict.
- "site staff do not have photographic memory" [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: a stated excuse for The System to "forget" context. In fiction only; the log remembers.
- "the site staff may chose not to respond if we feel the situation has been adequately explained." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: silence is a valid response, provided the log and the out-of-character screens still show the state and how it ends.
- "Every time a submission is modified by the user, its status in the moderation queue is 'reset'." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: a mechanic. Editing an appeal sends it to the back of the line. It is cruel but legible.
- The woodchuck entry: "784 butt cords of wood per day is the average... No wood is chucked on the 2nd of February..." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: the bureaucracy salts its own rulebook with one absurd entry, delivered in a straight voice. A model for one off-the-wall line inside a dry ruling.
- Queue states: Accepted, Queued and Declined. Declined means "deleted (save for its id number)." [Submissions_FAQ]
  - System: a three-state vocabulary for appeals. The "id number survives" detail rhymes with append-only: the record outlives the thing.

### 1.3 Ban-hammer one-liners

- A ban that turned a member's own complaint back on them was remembered as the perfect punishment line: quick, and in the offender's own words. It opened a themed monthly banlist. [a member page]
  - System: the ideal shape for a punishment line. It turns the player's own words back on them, and it is quick.
- A permaban justified by the length of the member's warn log, on the grounds that they had had every chance to change, read as fair to the community because the record spoke for itself. [a member page]
  - System: the "you had your chances" justification. The System can read the player's own log back to them.
- When a set of six themed duplicate accounts was permabanned, the moderator signed off with a pun on their theme and locked the thread. The community enjoyed that the verdict played along with the joke it ended. [Village_People]
  - System: a pun on the offenders' own theme, followed by a lock. The verdict plays along with the joke it ends.
- The forced signature (2006). A moderator punished an oversized signature image by replacing it with a confession in red capitals, written in the member's own voice. Some called it cruel; the punished member answered with a red signature of his own taunting the moderator back, others copied it, and the Big Red Sig became a fad: a punishment the community turned into a fashion. [a member page]
  - System: the shame-signature as a punishment. The System writes a confession onto the character, it is shown as a layer, and it expires. Note that the community turned it into a fashion.
- An admin once created a user group for a single banned member, named for what they had done, and moved the offending thread to the PPP as "gold". The community read the group as a sentence and a perverse honor at once. [a group page]
  - System: a punishment that is also an honor. The System can mint a bespoke title for a single offender.
- "even as far as PPP renames go, embedding Youtube videos in there is just dumb." [Walrus]
  - System: staff critiquing staff's own pranks. Mod Bots can grumble about each other.
- A member's ultimatum that he would not come back until a moderator was demodded was itself turned into board decoration, pasted beside the Walrus. Protest became part of the furniture. [Walrus]
  - System: the member's ultimatum. A stock line for a player's protest, which The System can ignore.

### 1.4 The officer who is not quite sure of his own ban

- "Contrary to the name, permanent bans are occasionally lifted, but this is very circumstantial." "Requests for indefinite bans are no longer honored." [Permaban]
  - System: "permanent" is a word in the fiction, not a fact of the code. This matches "No player is ever stuck". The System can say "forever" while the code says when it ends.
- The ban notices on banned members' pages share a voice. For a recurring offender who kept coming back and announcing himself, the page admits in a self-deprecating aside that the ban is permanent "or rather, we would like to think so", and the offense log reads as a loop ending "Rinse and repeat." [a member page]
  - System: the self-deprecating admission that bans do not hold. A good Mod Bot register for a recurring offender.
- Other notices take the formal finality voice ("the decision was made final") or end on a curt closing clause saying the member will not be returning. The community read these as the last word, whether or not they held. [member pages]
  - System: the formal finality voice and the curt closing clause. In the fiction only; the code still names a way out.
- "ADMIN NOTE: This information is most likely incredibly outdated. Use at your own risk." [a member page]
  - System: staff stamping a disclaimer onto a member's own page. A layer over a player's words, never a replacement for them.
- Banned members invented grades of punishment beyond what existed, claiming to be the victim of a "Superban". It was half grievance and half boast. [member pages]
  - System: members invent ranks of punishment beyond what exists. The System could make one real and bounded.

## 2. Governance mechanics: ranks, groups, warns, bans

### 2.1 Ranks

- **Founder** (Thunder Dragon). He is absent, settles disputes rarely, and handled a threatening rival site by politely contacting its webmaster. [Thunder_Dragon]
  - System: a dormant top authority whose rare interventions are mild.
- **Administrator**, **Global Moderator**, and **forum-specific Moderator**. [Forum_Admin, Forum_Moderator]
  - System: a three-tier ladder of Mod Bot personas.
- **CCC and PPP Mods.** Moderators of the spam board, with no ban power. [Forum_Moderator, Casual_Conversation_Castle, Pointless_Post_Palace]
  - System: a Mod Bot that can rename and move things but cannot punish.
- **Forum Janitor** (2007). Could edit topics and posts only. [Forum_Janitor]
  - System: a tidy-only persona.
- **Badge Moderator.** Tragic "declares himself" one on Dec 3 2007. [Badge_Moderator, Tragic]
  - System: self-declared ranks. A character could claim a title The System then honors or mocks.
- **Server Admin** (Nov 2007). Abandoned "due to confusion". [Server_Admin]
  - System: ranks that are invented and then dropped are part of the history.
- **Webmaster.** "not considered part of the conventional power hierarchy". [Webmaster]
  - System: the person who keeps the machine running, who stands outside the fiction's chain of command.
- **Tech Admin / Site Developer, Security Ninja, Wiki Sysop / Bureaucrat.** [Forum_Admin, Badges, Security_Ninja, Wiki_Sysop]
  - System: specialist offices. "Security Ninja" is the right register for a joke title.
- **Herald.** Could only post the Monthly News. [Herald]
  - System: a single-power office, like a persona whose only effect is the weekly announcement.
- **Event Leader.** Puppet accounts that staff log into: a shared account for hiring, a narrator account for storytelling and murder-mystery events, a shared holiday account for the gift exchange, a Halloween host account, and a staged villain account that ran a coup. One host account "doesn't actually have any Event Leader powers". [Event_Leader, event account pages]
  - System: the direct ancestor of Mod Bot personas. Several staff speak through one named mask, and one mask is all show and no power.
- **Social Media Manager, Quality Control, Main Site Mod/Admin.** [Main_Site_Staff, Timeline_of_MFGG]
  - System: later, more professional offices, from the "Major staff role restructure" of 2019.

### 2.2 Joke and special groups

- **Honorary Mod** (no powers) and **Fake Promotion** (the member bar without the powers). [Honorary_Mod, Fake_Promotion]
  - System: a reward that is a title with nothing behind it. It is cheap, harmless and very in character. The log should say what it is.
- **Friendly Icecream Truck / Ice Cream Man.** Its one power was posting embeds in the Lounge. [Friendly_Icecream_Truck]
  - System: a reward that grants one tiny, specific permission.
- **Greek Member.** Gave mod powers by accident until it was fixed. A dupe was made its only member. [Greek_Member]
  - System: a bug as lore. Divided World must not copy the bug, but the story of it is good flavor.
- **Time Travelling Ducks.** Lost in a migration, and "hilariously unlikely" to return. [Time_Travelling_Ducks]
  - System: groups lost to software changes are part of what the community mourns.
- **Members(2).** Created because one member's browser broke on upload fields. [Members_2_]
  - System: a bespoke group made to work around one person's problem, which shows the old guard improvising.
- **KFC Badge** for former admins. [Badges]
  - System: a retirement badge. A former persona or a retired character keeps a mark.

### 2.3 Restriction groups

- **Banned from PPP; Banned From PMs** (the mainsite "Supermute"); **Banned From User CP**, where recent profile changes were reverted; **Muted**. [Banned_from_PPP, Banned_From_PMs, Banned_From_User_CP, Muted]
  - System: punishments scoped to one feature, each a small and bounded effect. "Profile changes reverted" in Divided World must be a layer, never a deletion.
- **Validating.** Once used as a ban, since the confirmation email never arrives. [Validating]
  - System: a ban disguised as a pending state. Divided World may bluff in fiction, but the log must say plainly that it is a ban and when it ends.
- **Pending and Rejected** (the Trial Member pipeline); **Trial Member; Casual Member**. [Trial_Member, Pending, Rejected, Casual_Member]
  - System: see 2.4.
- **New Member** (2022). An anti-spambot group that lasts five posts. [New_Member]
  - System: a probation measured in Acts, not time.
- **Unbanned Leave.** A self-requested permaban, cancelled Nov 13 2007. [Unbanned_Leave, Permaban]
  - System: players asking to be punished. The old guard stopped honoring it, and The System might honor it as theater.

### 2.4 Membership tiers and probation

- **Casual Member.** Required 250 posts, two months without a warn, and the Happy Heart badge; later dropped to 2 months (Jan 12 2008). Removed Feb 9 2008 (the "Members -CCC" group), back May 28 2008, retired June 2009. [Casual_Member, Casual_Conversation_Castle]
  - System: gated rooms with thresholds that keep moving. Every gate must say how a player gets through it.
- **Trial Member** (Jun 12 2009). A 20-post trial, then Pending (reviewed in Forum 12), then pass or Rejected. A Rejected member was suspended for a year and then tried again. "Troublesome or controversial members" were to be sent back to trial. [Trial_Member, Timeline_of_MFGG]
  - System: a probation with a review and a retry. The structure works for a newcomer arc; a year-long wait is too long for a game.
- **Lounge entry** (2010). Required one of: a Happy Heart badge; a Super Submitter or Big Help badge with at most 20% warn; or 0% warn, 100+ posts and an account at least 2 months old. [The_Lounge]
  - System: a multi-path gate. Note the failure: too few could enter, the room died, and that fed The Split.
- **CCC compromise rules** (Feb 2008). The CCC became a General Chat subforum; a five-strike system was planned (three strikes for a temporary CCC ban, five for a permanent one); and everyone became a Casual Member by default. "The plan did not work however, and the CCC quickly degenerated into a second PPP." [Casual_Conversation_Castle]
  - System: a strike ladder scoped to one board, and evidence that loosening a gate brings the noise back.

### 2.5 The warn systems in order

- **EZBoard:** verbal warnings only. [Warn]
- **Invision:** 5 warns, then 3. The "3-heart system" restored hearts every 3 months, and once a year on Valentine's Day a heart was restored "just to be nice". [Warn]
  - System: hearts as lives. A dated amnesty such as the Valentine heart is a perfect holiday effect.
- **New warn system**, instated Feb to Apr 2006. Warn logs made public Aug 16 2006. [Warn, Timeline_of_MFGG]
  - System: public warn logs are the ancestor of the player's own log. Divided World's version is private to the player and always true.
- **phpBB:** a 30-point cap. 100% means a one-year suspension that "replaces the permaban", and the member returns at 90%. One point expires every 20 days. Automatic suspensions: 40% 1 day, 60% 2 days, 70% 4 days, 80% 7 days, 90% 10 days, 100% 1 year. Offense bands: small 1–3, medium 4–6, big 8–12, super 13–20. "The definition of small, medium, big, and super are up to the moderating team." [Warn]
  - System: the closest real precedent to "a fixed list of choices the code maps to an effect with limits". The bands are fixed, the decay is fixed, and the classification is left to judgment. That is exactly The System's job.
- **MFGG Improvement Initiative:** a "100 point system" in which warns expire faster. [MFGG_Improvement_Initiative]
  - System: faster decay as reform. Decay rates are a tunable that should be visible to the player.

### 2.6 Bans and their limits

- **High offenses (the old permaban list):** compromising server security, phishing, porn or warez, blackmail, duplicate accounts, repeated flaming or trolling, rapid repeated breaking of elementary rules, excessive spam, and severe report abuse. Being suspended within 24 hours of registering usually meant a permaban. [Permaban, Warn]
  - System: the real-moderation list. These belong to admins and the out-of-character layer, never to The System's in-game whims.
- **Duplicate accounts.** Banned, with exceptions for event accounts (which are "occasionally... banned as part of the event"), test accounts, and accounts made after technical problems. [Duplicate]
  - System: MFGG banned what Divided World makes the core of play. Several masks per player is the inversion of the old guard's top offense, and staff-run event dupes are the precedent.
- **The mask staff chose to keep.** A new account that became a beloved icon turned out to be a returning member. Staff had known all along, accepted the new account, and treated the original as the dupe; the mask fell only when its owner logged into the old account again and the new one was permabanned. The community felt the pleasure of a mask that held, and then the sting of how it fell. [a member page]
  - System: staff knowing who is behind a mask and choosing which mask is "real". Divided World's System must never act on ownership except in settled scoring.
- **One chance, then a permaban** policies: some members were let back on the condition that the next offense ended it. [member pages]
  - System: a last-chance probation state, which needs a stated end.
- **Year-bans as a ceiling.** Year-bans were "the highest penalty Vinny would agree to". [VinnyVideo]
  - System: a maximum penalty set by a softer administrator. A cap on The System's harshest effect.

### 2.7 Other mechanics

- **The 15-second flood limit**, which admins are exempt from. [Flood_limit]
  - System: rate limits apply to players, not to The System.
- **Secret forums.** Forum 6 (formerly 12), 27, 29, and the Admin Notepad. [Forum_6, Forum_27, Forum_29, Admin_Notepad]
  - System: see 1.5.
- **The Vault and the Archives.** The Vault is a read-only store of topics "considered important, influential, fun, or otherwise keepable", banlists among them. Every Archives subforum is locked. [The_Vault, Archives]
  - System: archived boards stay readable. A precedent for Divided World's archived boards with a stated way back.
- **The Cave of Flames** (a dumping ground) and the **Worthless Post Palace** (WPP), a read-only board for unworthy topics. People tried to get their topics sent to the WPP "until a rule was written against it". The WPP later closed "when it was found to be worthless itself". [Cave_of_Flames, Worthless_Post_Palace, Pointless_Post_Palace]
  - System: a punishment players chase as an honor. The System should expect players to game any shame-board.
- **Name changes.** An amnesty on May 28 2008 and a new name-change system on Dec 22 2009. Mega Man Name Changes became permanent, then the Great Mistake rollback undid them. [Mega_Man_Name_Changes, The_Great_Mistake, Timeline_of_MFGG]
  - System: renames as an event effect. A rename must be a layer that comes off.
- **The Green Button.** Members can fix the board themselves, and it is "rumoured that badges may be awarded". [The_Green_Button]
  - System: a rumored reward for an unexplained action. The System can let rumors about its rewards stand.
- **Custom Word Filter.** [Custom_Word_Filter]
  - System: autocorrect as an effect. It already exists in Divided World's plan as a layer.
- **Hiring events** run through a shared staff account for hiring, with letters of interest and tests. [an event account page]
  - System: a recruitment event voiced by a persona.
- **Demotion for inactivity** was routine. [Forum_Moderator, Timeline_of_MFGG]
  - System: personas or titles that lapse when unused.
- **MFGG 2.0:** two to four admins or mods approve each submission. [MFGG_2.0]
  - System: a quorum ruling. The System could stage a "panel" of Mod Bots that votes.
- **MFGG Spring Cleaning** (2010). Bulk removal of low-quality sprites; no games were removed. [MFGG_Spring_Cleaning]
  - System: a purge with a stated limit. Divided World's version hides and never destroys.
- **Review Drives, Game of the Month, and Monthly News** with "home made graphs". [Herald, Black_Squirrel, VinnyVideo]
  - System: recurring rituals. The weekly turnover is in this lineage.
- **A data-driven caretaker.** VinnyVideo ran the Who's MFGG? survey and the Better MFGG Plan. [VinnyVideo, Who_s_MFGG_]
  - System: an administration that asks. The System does not, which is the point.
- **The General Chat's "I don't know if this is General Chat worthy" policy.** Anyone who posted it would "receive a slap with a Cheep-Cheep." [General_Chat]
  - System: a comic punishment triggered by a phrase. A trigger-word effect The System could own.
- **MFGG Eclipse** (Apr 8 2024). A hacked admin account temporarily banned two staff and deleted two years of news. It was restored from backup, and staff powers were then stripped from inactive accounts. [Timeline_of_MFGG]
  - System: real damage from real compromise. It argues for append-only and for export that round-trips, and it belongs out of character.

## 3. Moderation as spectacle: board renames, closures, pranks

### 3.1 Renames as weather

- **The PPP's renames**, made "usually due to an admin's boredom, or because of a certain event". The list includes "Pointless Post SATAN SATAN SATAN SATAN SATAN" (for page 666), "Ravioli Time", "I AM A POINTLESS POST PIRATE", "U CAN'T POST THIS", "PPP: We do what we must, because we can", "Pointless Toast Palace", "Pointless WIP Palace" (the board "broke" with giant cracks after a pruning), and Hell, with banner and background changed to match. The Walrus renames came "almost daily" during E3 2006. [Pointless_Post_Palace, Walrus]
  - System: the direct precedent for The System renaming weekly boards. Renames track the news and the board's own milestones.
- **The CCC's renames.** "Casual Communist Castle", "Casual Conversation Ghost Town" (expected to empty after Brawl's release; "It didn't."), "Bad Wolf", "Ca.ual Con.ersa.ion Cas.le" decaying to ".a.u.....n....a.i...C.s..e" and then "..........................", "Hardcore Horseplay Hallway", and "Pointless Post Palace (Just Kidding)". [Casual_Conversation_Castle]
  - System: a name that erodes letter by letter is a superb turnover effect. The board's name decays across the week.
- **A Day That Will Live in Infamy** (Mar 28 2007). Klobber and ShadowMan, both admins, fought a rename war: "Pointless Post Palace? This is SPARTAAAAAAAAAAA", "Paintless Post Palace", "ZION, THE WAR IS OVER" (with a WWII background and "We Are The Champions"). ShadowMan won. The board was closed and replaced with Dr. Wily and "Never Forget 3/28". [A_Day_That_Will_Live_in_Infamy, Pointless_Post_Palace]
  - System: two Mod Bot personas fighting over a board's name in public. The fight itself is the event.
- **Land of the Hoff** (May 29 2007). ShadowMan replaced a "Road Work Ahead" sign with pictures of David Hasselhoff that multiplied until "you could not see the threads or even reply". [Pointless_Post_Palace]
  - System: an effect that briefly blocks the board. It needs a short, stated end, as this one had.
- **The WPP renamed by Trasher** to "for sale" and "The Cave of Lames". [Worthless_Post_Palace]
  - System: renaming a shame-board to mock it further.
- **The PPP description slot.** The Walrus, and "JESUS CHRIST, GET AWAY FROM THAT WALRUS!" [Walrus]
  - System: the board's subtitle as a second channel for The System.

### 3.2 Closures, prunes and countdowns

- **Stupidity Discharge** (Jun 8 2006). A Lost-style countdown at PPP page 996, then whiteout and closure, a "Pointless Fallout" board, and a prune that crashed the SQL server and purged half the PPP. [Stupidity_Discharge]
  - System: a staged apocalypse for a board. Divided World keeps the drama and drops the purge, so the old posts survive.
- **Pointless Supernova** (Aug 31 2006). A replacement board where only mods could post. [Pointless_Supernova]
  - System: a board where only The System speaks for a stated time.

### 3.3 Event storylines run by staff

- **Life on MFGG** (Aug 8 2008). A staff-run account "warned" of a time disturbance and was banned. The CCC's name then faded letter by letter, the Walrus collapsed into a time hole, and the board was reskinned as 1973. A second account claiming to be from the year 4625 convinced members to "kill" the first, and it then turned out to be a henchman of the Walrus. The CCC was "capsulized", with a redirect to an animation. [Life_on_MFGG, Casual_Conversation_Castle]
  - System: a full arc in which Mod Bots lie, the members are tricked into the wrong side, and the board itself is the casualty. This is The System's genre at full strength.
- **The Halloween 2010 Lounge murder mystery.** The board was opened to all and renamed "Horrifying Halloween Hallway". A narrator persona told the story; each "killed" member got a ghost member bar; and clues pointed to a red herring before the culprit was revealed as an event account. Members could take spooky names. [The_Lounge]
  - System: event-only states with a set end ("open to all until the afternoon... of the 1st of November"). A ghost bar is a fine bounded effect.
- **THE VOID.** Created as a "jail" for complainers. Few rules were broken, so Black Squirrel started "capturing people", running the Dalek accounts himself. [THE_VOID]
  - System: a jail that ran short of inmates and began abducting them. A model for an in-game jail with a stated release.
- **The Great Mistake** (Feb 18 2008). After a prank handed real power to joke accounts and the board was wrecked, the admin left in charge posted a stand-by notice with a joking apology. During the restore, that admin imposed a rule that every post must contain "meatballs". A member who refused to say the "M word" was renamed "super duper meatballs". The community remembered the edict more fondly than the wreck. [The_Great_Mistake]
  - System: a mandatory word as a temporary rule, with a rename as the penalty for refusing. A precise template for a bounded System edict.

From Timeline_of_MFGG unless another page is cited.

## 7. Implications for The System

A synthesis of the "System:" lines above.

1. **The register is bureaucratic deadpan with sudden absurdity.** The Submissions FAQ ("No.", "MEDIOCRITY IS A BAD THING", the woodchuck entry) and "How crushing." show the target voice: short, capitalized when cross, never apologetic, and occasionally deranged. [Submissions_FAQ, Friday_12th_2009]
2. **Punishment lines turn the player's words back on them.** A ban line that turned the member's own complaint back on them, and the pun on a dupe set's own theme, show the form. A model should be prompted to quote the offense back. [a member page, Village_People]
3. **"Permanent" is a fiction word.** MFGG's own pages admit permabans are lifted and bans do not hold. The System may say "forever"; the log states the real end. [Permaban, a member page]
4. **The phpBB warn table is the model for bounded effects.** Fixed bands, fixed decay and fixed suspensions, with "up to the moderating team" covering only the classification. That is exactly the split between the code's list and the model's judgment. [Warn]
5. **Renaming boards is the old guard's signature move.** Its forms are renames that track the news, renames that decay letter by letter, rename wars between admins, and holiday renames of the whole site. All of them map directly to the weekly turnover. [Pointless_Post_Palace, Casual_Conversation_Castle, A_Day_That_Will_Live_in_Infamy, April_Fool_s_Prank]
6. **Mod Bot personas have a precedent.** Event Leader accounts were masks that several staff spoke through, one of them all show and no power. Staff also ran storyline accounts that lied to the members. [Event_Leader, Life_on_MFGG, The_Lounge]
7. **Bounded edicts.** The "meatballs" rule, the one-hour "resistance" bans, the Valentine heart, the Cheep-Cheep slap, and the Dan Schneider vandalism are each a small, timed, legible effect. [The_Great_Mistake, April_Fool_s_Prank, Warn, General_Chat]
8. **Jails and shame-boards get gamed.** Players sought the WPP until a rule stopped it, THE VOID ran out of inmates, and the Big Red Sig became a fad. Every shame effect should expect to become a status symbol. [Worthless_Post_Palace, THE_VOID, a member page]
9. **Democracy versus top-down is the community's old wound.** "MFGGers wanted democracy instead of the top down approach." The System is top-down by design, and it can say so in character. The protest that reversed the CCC closure shows how players can still win something back. [Friday_12th_2009, Casual_Conversation_Castle]
10. **Where MFGG went wrong, Divided World's rules already differ.**
    - MFGG banned duplicate accounts; here they are the game.
    - MFGG's prunes purged posts; here nothing a player wrote is destroyed.
    - A prank chain ended in a real demotion, and a joke account deleted a board; here The System's effects are bounded by code.
    - Hacks and deletions lost history twice; here export round-trips.
    - Gated rooms died when too few could enter; here every state names its way out.
    [Duplicate, Stupidity_Discharge, Timeline_of_MFGG, The_Great_Mistake, an event page, The_Lounge]
11. **The Split is the origin myth of the audience.** Its poll split nearly in half, and the community divided across two forums. The instance's own name points at it. A turnover that divides a board, or a Mod Bot that remembers "the Split", speaks directly to this audience. [The_Split, Timeline_of_MFGG]
