# MFGG wiki, lane B: forums, boards and events

Record, written 2026-10-30, from the MFGG wiki as read on that date. What was read, and how people are treated, is in
`README.md` here; The System's voice, drawn from all four lanes, is in `system-voice.md`.

A research record mined from the local dump of the MFGG wiki. It covers the 193 pages in lane B, plus a few leads
taken from the rest of the dump (Timeline of MFGG, Minus World, The Split, Event Leader, Greek Member, Forum Janitor,
Walrus, MFGG Improvement Initiative, Review Drive). Every item cites its page title in
[brackets]. Each item ends with a **DW:** line that suggests how it could become a weekly board, a Random Event, a card
or a System effect in Divided World.

Conventions:

- People are recorded by their public role (admin, moderator, member, server admin, event account) and by what they
  did on the forum. Real names, ages, locations and personal details are left out, including the aggregate
  demographics in [Who's MFGG?].
- Items that center on one non-staff individual are marked **(individual: owner's call)**.
- Event accounts are staged personas run by staff. They are described by what they did (a narrator account, a host
  account, a shared holiday account), not by their usernames.
- An infamous incident (a ban, a dupe caught, a troll, a flamewar, phishing, a hacked account, someone mocked, a member
  called infamous, a game ridiculed) names no individual, member or staff. It is written in the abstract: what happened
  in general terms, what the community felt about it, and what that suggests for the design. A page title that is a
  person's name is cited as [a member page].
- Where an adaptation would collide with a rule in this repository's CLAUDE.md (no destroying a player's words, no
  stuck states, no response computed from a private fact, the log never lies), the DW line says so.

## 1. Board eras and the shape of the forum

- **Software eras.** The forum ran on a guestbook, then EZBoard ("EZBread", with pop-ups), then vBulletin. The
  vBulletin board ran from February to October 2003, on a borrowed host, with an unlicensed copy; it was pulled, and
  for a while MFGG had neither a site nor a board. Invision/IPB followed from October 11, 2003 (all but a few members
  re-registered two days later). phpBB ran from November 7 or 9, 2009 ("MFGG 3.0") and was closed on November 15, 2010
  for The Split. "MFGG 5.5" phpBB opened on November 26, 2010, installed over the old one and so holding traces of it
  (old staff topics, warn logs, the MFGGreece skin). MyBB opened on November 27, 2017. [Mario Fan Games Galaxy
  Forums] [EZBoard] [VBulletin] [Invision] [PhpBB] [MyBB] [Timeline of MFGG]
  **DW:** "Eras" are a theme the System can impose for a week. A *Legacy Skin* weekly board renders in a dead
  software's chrome (EZBoard pop-ups, IPB "Who's Viewing", phpBB "MFGG 3.0"), as a theme layer only.
- **Migration rules.** At the phpBB move, previous bans were declared void because of backlogs. For one week,
  usernames were "protected": an existing member could contest a claim to their name. Badges were reassigned, and
  some features (custom filters) never came back. Both forums ran side by side for a few days. [PhpBB]
  **DW:** A *Jubilee* Random Event lifts every active board ban for a stated window and logs that it did so. A *Name
  Protection* rule matters at any instance reset or import.
- **Category structure.** Over the eras the boards sat in categories: MFGG (news, fangame boards), Community (General
  Chat, Arts, Gaming, Help) and "Fun Stuff". Fun Stuff was renamed "The Dungeon" on June 22, 2007, and held the PPP,
  then the CCC, General Friendliness, Forum Games, The Lounge, the Archives and the Competitions. The page judges that
  "a more autocratic structure seemed to only inspire drama and hostility." [Mario Fan Games Galaxy Forums] [Fun
  Stuff] [Pointless Post Palace]
  **DW:** "The Dungeon" is a ready-made name for the System's basement category, where weekly and event boards live
  below the permanent ones.
- **The Split (November 2010).** Admins split the fangame boards from the community boards. The community moved to a
  new forum, Minus World, which opened November 15, 2010; MFGG reopened fangame-only on November 26. The community
  was roughly half for and half against. The Lounge stayed open (and posted in, often critically) for months, then
  was locked. Event leaders apparently can still post there. [The Split] [PhpBB] [The Lounge] [Minus World]
  **DW:** This is Divided World's founding myth. A *Schism* Random Event could split one board into two boards that
  each keep half the threads, with a vote to reunite.
- **Minus World's own reinvention (2016).** A "Good Sh*t" metric was proposed "to reward users for posting enjoyable
  content". The relaunch (MyBB 1.8, September 11, 2016) aimed "to create a site that is just as much of a game as it
  is a forum" and is "half-forum-half-fighting game with remarkably lax moderation". [Minus World] (lead outside the
  lane)
  **DW:** This is direct precedent for KayfaBB. The "Good Sh*t" metric is an Aura-like public reward score.

### 2.3 Other casual and off-topic boards

- **Worthless Post Palace (WPP), June 21 to September 6, 2007.** A read-only dump for PPP topics judged unworthy.
  Members abused it by trying to get their topics moved there, until a rule stopped it. Its names were "for sale" in
  mid-July 2007 and "WPP: The Cave of Lames". It was deleted when it was "found to be worthless itself".
  [Worthless Post Palace] [Pointless Post Palace]
  **DW:** A *Worthless* weekly board where the System moves threads it judges unworthy, read-only. Moving is a layer;
  the posts survive and can be moved back. Players who chase the move are the joke.
- **Pointless Supernova (August 31 to September 3, 2006).** It replaced the PPP during the third prune, but the PPP
  was not actually pruned. Logged-out visitors still saw the PPP, and only staff and one member could post.
  [Pointless Supernova]
  **DW:** A *Decoy Board* effect: logged-in players see a stand-in board while the real one is shown to visitors.
- **Pointless Fallout / Stupidity Discharge (June 8, 2006).** At page 996, a *Lost*-style countdown of the pages left
  to 1,000 appeared on every page with sirens. A member made page 1,000. The PPP whited out, a discharge sound played,
  and the title became "Stupidity Discharge". "Pointless Fallout" opened in its place. The prune crashed the host's SQL
  server, taking down sister sites; 15 minutes later half the PPP came back and half was purged. [Stupidity
  Discharge]
  **DW:** The *Countdown* Random Event: announce a board's end at a page number, count it down on every page, and let
  the player whose post lands on the number trigger it. The purge must be a layer (archived, recoverable), never a
  deletion.
- **General Chat (GC).** The "serious" board. One policy said anyone who posted "I don't know if this is General Chat
  worthy" got slapped with a Cheep-Cheep. After the CCC closed, its lenient spam policy brought inconsistent
  moderation and drama, which the page counts among the causes of The Split. "General Chat Strikes Back" reopened it
  on November 18, 2012. [General Chat]
  **DW:** The Cheep-Cheep slap is a perfect small System punishment card, *Slapped*: a fish icon on the post for a
  day.
- **The Lounge (July 13, 2010 to locked after The Split).** Conceived as the "Community Fridge". Entry needed one of:
  a Happy Heart; a Super Submitter or Big Help badge with 20% warn or less; or 0% warn, 100+ posts and an account at
  least 2 months old. It hosted the Joke Game Contests. For events it was renamed, reskinned and opened to all; on
  Halloween 2010 it became the "Horrifying Halloween Hallway" with the Booberry skin forced on. It never got active and
  was seen as "General Chat 2". [The Lounge] [MFGG Improvement Initiative]
  **DW:** An *Open House* effect: a gated board opens to everyone for the length of an event, then closes again.
- **Space Junk Galaxy / Cosmic Trash Nebula (July 9, 2017 onward).** Opened as a trial for the 16th anniversary,
  then made permanent and renamed. Rules: "casual but not pointless"; "Short posts and reaction images are OK, but
  utter gibberish is not"; "Don't go bonkers and create a dozen new topics in a few minutes." [Cosmic Trash Nebula]
  [Pointless Post Palace]
  **DW:** A model rule text for a casual weekly board, in the System's out-of-character voice.
- **Forum32x (February 14, 2010 to fall 2010).** An outside "Bad Forum" spawned during an MFGG outage, with a rule
  forbidding topics about MFGG. It peaked on April 1 as "Pizzatendo". [Forum32x]
  **DW:** A *Refuge* Random Event: when a board is closed, the System opens a rival board that forbids mention of the
  closed one.
- **TempFGG (February 14, 2010).** A stand-in board a moderator set up while the server was down; irrelevant within
  hours. [TempFGG]

### 2.5 Archives, The Vault and the Prunery

- **Archives.** A Dungeon forum made only of locked sub-forums: The Vault, the competition archives, The Midas

- **The Vault.** Read-only to members, and full control for staff. It stores good topics, events, banlists (on IPB)

  **DW:** A *Vaulted* thread state: the System or a vote saves a thread from a board's weekly turnover.
- **Prunery.** A staff-only sub-forum in the Vault, made when one member asked for certain posts to be removed. That
  member provided an SQL script to do it. "Unlikely to be used again." **(individual: owner's call)** [Prunery]
  **DW:** This is where a player's request to hide their own words would live. In DW that is a layer over the words,
  never a deletion.

## 3. Secret, password and staff forums

- **Forum 6 (formerly Forum 12): "The Mod-Staff Super Forum".** Hidden and "heralded as a great secret", with crude
  rumors about what goes on there. Forum 27's purpose was never stated publicly. Forum 29 was secret, for staff and
  "Operation CLAM". Forum 39 was admin-only: "It seems that no one knows what forbidden arts are practiced within the
  darkest depths of MFGG." Forum 41, created late 2013, was "It's a secret to everybody!" [Forum 6] [Forum 27] [Forum
  29] [Forum 39] [Forum 41]
  **DW:** Numbered locked boards that show only as a number in the index are good mystique for the System. They must
  never confirm who can see them (that would be a private fact about staff or ownership).
- **The Mothership.** Added to Fun Stuff for one night; only people who solved the password saw inside. [The
  Mothership] [Fun Stuff]
  **DW:** *One Night Only*, a Random Event board that exists for 12 hours behind a riddle.
- **SPECTRE (February 2006).** A PPP sub-forum with a James Bond skin, which reset members' skins to the default. The
  password "JAWS" was "fairly easy to crack" and was posted around. [SPECTRE]
- **Really Secret Forum (May 26, 2007).** An admin's joke PPP sub-forum whose description gave clues: "7 letters
  long", "the same word repeated 3 times" (sic), "has the letter b", "not supposed to be edible to humans",
  "nautically-related". The password was tugboattugboattugboat. Inside, one topic said the 10th poster wins a prize. It
  was deleted after most people got in. [Really Secret Forum]
  **DW:** A *Riddle Door* card. The prize goes to the Nth poster, not the first, which rewards timing over speed.
- **Gehn's 233rd Age (August 2, 2005).** An admin's password-guessed forum (password "wahrk"). Inside, members were
  told to get one thread to 25,000 views. "It only reached 6670 before Gehn's 233rd Age was permanently sealed off",
  even to admins. [Gehn's 233rd Age]
  **DW:** A *Collective Goal* Random Event with a deadline and a consequence for failing. In DW the failure seals the
  board to new posts, but the board stays readable (no stuck state; nothing destroyed).
- **Ωffhand Ωration Ωlympus (April 1, 2010).** A PPP/CCC-like board behind a long quiz about Greece, which most users
  abandoned. Those who passed joined the "Greek Member" group. [Ωffhand Ωration Ωlympus] [Greek Member]
- **The Hallowsphere (Halloween 2015).** Three nested password layers (Shallow, Medial, Deep). Each layer's clues were
  posted in the layer above, and the last key was four chunks, each with its own clue. The first person into the Deep
  layer got a golden skull badge, and everyone else who entered got a normal skull. The Shallow layer stayed open for
  months until a moderator locked it and removed the passwords. [The Hallowsphere]
  **DW:** This is the strongest model for a multi-week System puzzle: nested boards, one reward for the first in and
  a lesser one for everyone after.
- **Easter Egg Hunt boards: Meadow Planet (2014) and Marsh Planet (2015).** A temporary GC sub-forum held five
  password-locked "egg" forums with three puzzles each. Answers were typed **into the forum's search box** to reveal
  the next puzzle. The first poster in an egg forum won a prize and could decline it, passing it to the next poster.
  Every finder got an Egg Hunter badge, and finding all five gave Egg Hunter Pro. New puzzles came on each of the
  first five days. Entry passwords played on themselves ("THEPASSWORDISNOTHOW" → "NOTHOW", "NOTEGG"). One answer hid
  in a member's avatar, and puzzles were hidden in posts across the whole forum. Stats were kept: most solvers 18,
  fastest 24 minutes, 56 cracks, 1,750 collective minutes. [Easter Egg Hunt] [Meadow Planet] [Marsh Planet]
  **DW:** *Search Is the Key*: the System hides answers that only the forum's own search reveals. The decline-and-pass
  prize is a nice touch. Hiding a clue in a character's avatar is play; it must not leak who owns the avatar.

## 4. Jails, dungeons, penalty forums and penalty groups

- **Cave of Flames.** The dump for flamewars, later staff-only, listed among obsolete boards as one "which caused more
  harm than good". Aliases: Chernobyl, Ravenholm, Direbo. During the Great Mistake rollback, staff sent the Battle
  Without Honor or Humility event there ("I'll be Black"). [Cave of Flames] [Mario Fan Games Galaxy Forums] [Battle
  Without Honor or Humility]
  **DW:** A *Flame Pit* weekly board where the System moves heated threads. It is read-only and visible, and it names
  its way out (a mod bot releases a thread after N days of quiet).
- **Joliet State Penitentiary (JSP).** An alternate forum for problem PPP posters: "Think of it as Pointless Post
  Probation. If you're a continued problem, you'll just get time in JSP." Described as "Unspeakable horrors"; it only
  ever held one inmate. [Joliet State Penitentiary]
  **DW:** *Probation*, a jail board a character is sent to for a stated term. It can post only there and can read
  everywhere. This is the canonical System punishment, and its log entry states the term.
- **THE VOID (April 1, 2009).** At the end of Life on MFGG, the CCC was "capsulized", and a purple vortex drew "a
  selected group of Casual Members into THE VOID with no way of escape". It had archaic topic icons, a purple motif
  and a music embed. Members could visit and talk to the prisoners. Then it swallowed every board, MFGG section last,
  until at 7:54 PM it vanished and everything returned "as if nothing had happened". [THE VOID] [April Fool's Prank]
  **DW:** *The Void* Random Event: selected characters are pulled into a jail board others can visit, then the jail
  spreads board by board and ends at a stated time. "No way of escape" is the fiction; the log states the end time
  (the fiction may lie, the log never does).
- **A user group for one offender.** An admin created a group, named for what one banned member had done, and moved
  the offending thread to the PPP as "gold". The community took the group as a sentence and a perverse honor at once.
  [a group page]
  **DW:** A humiliating member group is a punishment card idea (*Dunce Cap* title), but only if it is timed and has an
  appeal.
- **Skull badge for banned members**, later reused for Halloween participants. [Badges] [The Hallowsphere]
- **Ghost Member / "Deceased" member bars.**
  - *The Murderous MFGGer*: killed members got a "Ghost Member" group and stopped posting in the topic.
  - *Nyerk nyerk nyerk!*: killed members wore a "Deceased" bar.
  - *MFGG Mafia*: a dead player "chose to persist as a ghost".

  [The Murderous MFGGer] [Nyerk nyerk nyerk!] [MFGG Mafia]
  **DW:** *Ghosted* is a character state with a visible bar. It needs a way out: it ends with the event or after a
  set time.
- **MFGG Football's bench.** Banned members' players "sit on the bench". [MFGG Football]
  **DW:** A suspended character still appears in team or roster events, benched, which keeps the mask present.
- **Trial Member system (Friday 12th 2009, June 12, 2009).**
  - New members were limited to 20 posts, then assessed in the staff forum.
  - Failing meant a one-year suspension, and troublesome existing members were sent *back* to trial.
  - The first complaint came at 3h45m, then mass complaints; everyone sent back was sent forward, and an admin was
    replaced.
  - An IPB glitch deleted an account. "So it was a 'can't do right without doing wrong' situation and is further proof
    that MFGG does not know what it wants."

  [Friday 12th 2009]

## 11. Mechanics worth stealing

- **Post counts.** PPP and Dungeon posts did not count, and Challenge posts were corrected by hand. A joke account had
  an admin-set count of 1,000,000. Access gates use counts (the CCC needed 250, the Lounge 100+). [Pointless Supernova]
  [Forum Games] [The Face: Event] [Casual Conversation Castle] [The Lounge]
  **DW:** Counts are per character. A gate counted across a player's masks would prove shared ownership.
- **Page counters as triggers.** Prunes at about 1,000 pages, the 666-page SATAN rename, the page-996 countdown. [Pointless
  Post Palace] [Stupidity Discharge]
- **Flood limit.** 15 seconds between posts (and searches), admins exempt, disabled in event forums. Several
  Challenges turned it off on purpose. [Flood limit] [Challenges]
  **DW:** *Flood Gate* card: a board's flood limit set to zero, or to a minute, for a day.
- **Warn systems.**
  - EZBoard: verbal warnings only.
  - IPB: five "hearts", later three, lasting 3 months. Hearts were restored every three months, and "once a year on
    Valentine's day, a heart would be restored just to be nice". Warn levels were visible to all, and warn logs
    became public on August 16, 2006. Later came timed bans of hours up to joke lengths.
  - phpBB points: a maximum of 30; one point expires every 20 days. Reaching the maximum is an automatic one-year
    suspension ("This replaces the permaban"), and you return at 90%. Each new warning at a high level auto-suspends:
    40% for 1 day, 60% for 2, 70% for 4, 80% for 7, 90% for 10, 100% for 1 year. Offense bands: small 1-3, medium
    4-6, big 8-12, super 13-20. "Except in special situations, users will be suspended by the warning system, not by
    a moderator."
  - The 2010 Initiative proposed a 100-point system.

  [Warn] [MFGG warn system] [Timeline of MFGG] [MFGG Improvement Initiative]
  **DW:** This is the closest real precedent for a System punishment ladder with stated exits: points that decay on a
  clock, fixed thresholds, a machine that suspends rather than a person. The *Valentine's heart* is a lovely amnesty
  Random Event.
- **Badges.**
  - Badges stack with a count (capped at 5+ on IPB).
  - The Happy Heart is lost on any warning (tiers at 2 months, 6 months and 2 years without one); it took 100 posts
    and membership throughout. The Ultra Happy Heart came in 2009.
  - The Skull badge went to banned members, then to Halloween players.
  - The KFC badge went to retired admins.
  - "Most Helpful Dictator" was a joke badge passed around and bribed for 5 coins.
  - Anniversary badges went to those who wished MFGG happy birthday.
  - The Reporter badge was for proper use of the report button.
  - Others: Egg Hunter and Pro, Event Winner, Grammar Game Winner, Good Citizen (Secret Santa elves), E3 Bingo Winner,
    golden and normal skulls, a Leek badge.
  - A self-declared "Badge Moderator" appeared in 2007.
  - Before real badges, members made image badges for each other's signatures.

  [Badges] [Timeline of MFGG] [The Hallowsphere] [Easter Egg Hunt]
