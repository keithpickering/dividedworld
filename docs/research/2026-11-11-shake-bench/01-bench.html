Shake Bench

Flick-scroll the thread fast (a phone swipe, a trackpad fling, or the Flick buttons) and glitter shakes off the Glitter Bomb posts in view. It flies the way the page was moving, tumbles and glints as air drag catches it, and settles. Wherever it comes down on a post, any post, it stays there and scrolls with that post. Anywhere else (the margins, the gaps between posts) it lands on the page itself and scrolls with the page. With a mouse, pass over a bombed post to pick glitter up, then move away to drop it.

Switch Shake to "Today's" to compare with what the build does now.
