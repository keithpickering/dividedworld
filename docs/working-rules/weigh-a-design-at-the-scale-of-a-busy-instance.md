# Weigh a design at the scale of a busy instance

Incident. Rewritten in the design session of 2026-10-28 (docs/plans/2026-10-28-kit-step-1-design.md) from currents' rule "Weigh a design at the scale of a real workspace, not the size of today's test pool" (carried at f6aa96d), with an instance as the unit of scale.

The owner's words. "The system should scale effectively and not rely on low numbers to achieve reasonable performance: a lot of users may become a gameplay problem, it shouldn't become an architectural one" (the owner, 2026-10-28).
