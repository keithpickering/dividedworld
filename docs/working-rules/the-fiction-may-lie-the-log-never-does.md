# The fiction may lie; the log never does

Incident. Rewritten in the design session of 2026-10-28 (docs/plans/2026-10-28-kit-step-1-design.md) from currents' rule "Write for a member's understanding, not for the most specific term" (carried at f6aa96d). Followed to the letter, that rule would have flattened The System's in-character voice into plain, honest words; the owner chose to let the fiction bluff and to keep a player's own log true.

The owner's words. Asked whether The System may lie to players, the owner chose "the fiction may lie; the log never does" ("A sounds good", the owner, 2026-10-28), beside "there is always an opportunity to step back and appreciate that a game is being played."
