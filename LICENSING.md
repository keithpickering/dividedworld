This is the one map of who may do what with what is in this repository. It is current, and it points to the lists that
hold the details instead of copying them. The terms are the owner's choice, written carefully; they are not legal
advice, and a lawyer's read is worth having before anyone relies on them beyond a fan community's reuse.

## The engine: AGPL-3.0-or-later

The full text is `LICENSE`. Anyone may use, change, share and sell it. Whoever receives a copy gets the source under the
same license, and whoever runs a modified KayfaBB for users must offer those users its source (AGPL section 13).

These paths are the engine: `engine/`, `supabase/`, `tools/`, `web/`, `e2e/`, `scripts/`, the root configuration files,
and the repository's own documents and skills (`docs/`, `specs/`, `.claude/`, `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `PRODUCT.md`,
`spec.md`). The engine's own pictures, such as the card template, are under the same terms.

## Divided World: AGPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY 4.0, your choice

Everything Divided World made in `instances/divided-world/` (its code, its words, its pictures, its prompts and its
personas) is offered under the AGPL-3.0-or-later or under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode), whichever you pick. The preferred credit is
"Divided World (https://dw.gripe)". The instance's own statement is `instances/divided-world/LICENSE.md`.

The card art already published under CC0 stays CC0, and a CC0 dedication cannot be taken back from copies already out.
New art may name its own terms in its `art.json`; art offered without other terms comes under the dual offer above.

## Files with their own terms

Some files are not ours to license, or carry terms of their own: fonts, sounds, photographs from Wikimedia Commons,
and some fan art. Where a file's terms differ from its layer's, they are declared beside it, in an `art.json`, a
`sounds.json`, a `CREDITS.md` or a `licenses.json` (`engine/licenses.json`, `instances/divided-world/licenses.json`).
`tools/check-licenses.mjs` refuses a media file that none of them declares. Each instance's License & credits page, at
`/license`, lists them for players.

Fan art that uses Nintendo's characters or marks does not license those, and the Snuckles image grants no rights in the
Snuckles character.

## What is not offered

`docs/research/` holds captures of other sites and works made for research. They are records, kept for what they show,
and are not offered under any license here.

## Names

Neither license grants the names "KayfaBB" or "Divided World". Forks are welcome and may say they are based on KayfaBB or
Divided World, but may not present themselves as the official KayfaBB or Divided World.

## Contributing

A contribution is licensed under the terms of the folder it lands in, or the terms its own declaration names. See
`CONTRIBUTING.md`.
